Nursery

Where curiosity becomes confidence.

For ages 2 to 5, learning is active and language-rich, with early literacy, numeracy, creativity, movement and social development working together.

Play with purpose

Young children learn by doing, talking and trying again.

Nursery learning balances guided teaching with hands-on exploration. Children begin to recognise sounds, symbols, patterns and quantities while continuing to build independence and confidence.

They also practise the social habits that make learning possible: listening, sharing, taking turns, following routines and expressing needs respectfully.

Learning areas

Early foundations that prepare children for Primary.

The aim is readiness without pressure, with developmentally appropriate expectations and plenty of space for imagination.

Early literacy

Listening, speaking, sound awareness, letter recognition, vocabulary and early reading behaviours.

Early numeracy

Counting, quantity, patterns, shapes, sorting and simple problem solving through practical activities.

Creative expression

Art, music, movement, storytelling and role play give children multiple ways to communicate ideas.

Personal development

Independence, responsibility, kindness, confidence and emotional awareness are practised every day.
