Our School

Small enough to know every child. Ambitious enough to prepare them well.

NOW Foundation School was established in 2024 to provide a caring and academically purposeful foundation from Creche through Primary.

Our direction

A school should do more than fill a timetable. It should help children build competence, character, confidence and a genuine love of learning.

We want children to leave each stage stronger than they entered it.

That means strong academic foundations, but also habits that matter beyond examinations: listening, speaking clearly, thinking independently, working with others, taking responsibility and treating people with respect.

Our vision

To raise confident, capable and principled young people who are prepared to learn, lead and contribute positively wherever they go.

Our mission

To provide structured, child-centred education that develops knowledge, character, curiosity and faith through thoughtful teaching and supportive relationships.

What guides us

  • Every child deserves to be known, respected and challenged appropriately.
  • Good teaching should make children think, not simply repeat.
  • Families and school achieve more when communication is clear and consistent.
  • Character is built through daily practice, example and accountability.
  • Faith should be reflected in compassion, integrity, discipline and service.
A learning environment that feels secure

Care, structure and safeguarding belong together.

Children learn best when they feel safe, understood and able to ask for help. Our approach is built around respectful adult-child relationships, age-appropriate routines and clear expectations.

Parents are encouraged to share relevant information about a child's health, learning needs and development so that the school can respond appropriately and communicate responsibly.
